# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the InboxPilot community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.
- Protect private email, identity, and security information encountered while
  helping contributors or self-hosters.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing mailbox content, credentials, logs, or security details that could
  harm a user.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
will investigate fairly, respect the reporter's privacy, and may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently
exclude participants when necessary.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1,
available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
